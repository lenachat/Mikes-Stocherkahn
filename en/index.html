Good to know

How many people fit in a Stocherkahn?

Up to 26 people fit in one Stocherkahn. If needed, several boats can be booked at the same time.

How long does a tour take?

A Stocherkahn tour takes about 60 minutes, covering roughly 2 km around the Tübingen Neckar island. It can be extended on request.

What happens if it rains?

We offer a good-weather guarantee: if it rains within 30 minutes before your tour, you can cancel or reschedule free of charge with a quick phone call.

Can I eat and drink on the boat?

Yes, you are welcome to bring your own food and drinks!

When can I go?

Stocherkahn tours run daily from March to October. Exact times can be arranged individually.

Where does the Stocherkahn dock?

There are several landing stages in Tübingen. Shared tours always start at the Hölderlinturm. For all other tours, the starting point can be arranged individually. Options include the Hölderlinturm, Casinospitze, Hotel Domizil or the Neckarmüller.
